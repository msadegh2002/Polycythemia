POLYCYTHEMIA - PRESENTATION REVIEW

1. Right-click the ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click Open Presentation.cmd.

The presentation opens in your browser, with its animations and transitions.
No installation or internet connection is needed.

Space / Right arrow: next step or slide
Left arrow: previous step or slide
F: fullscreen

Close the presentation tab when finished. Keep the extracted files together.

For Windows 10/11, 64-bit. A portable runtime is included in the viewer folder.
